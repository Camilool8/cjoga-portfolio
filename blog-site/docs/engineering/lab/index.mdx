# KodePull cluster

KodePull's on-prem cluster is six nodes of K3s on a mix of Proxmox and bare metal. It runs the workloads of KodePull clients who want their data kept on local hardware, relays to and backs up KodePull's production clusters in the cloud and in datacenters, and hosts `cjoga.cloud` and `kodepull.com`. It started as my homelab, and the architecture took three iterations to settle.
