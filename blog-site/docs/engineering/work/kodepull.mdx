KodePull SRL is the company Fernando Reyes and I founded in 2025. Where the other pages here cover work I've done for other people's organizations, this is the one I help own. I'm the CTO. Fernando is the CEO.

The public brand is **KodePull** and the marketing site lives at [kodepull.com](https://kodepull.com/en/).

## What we build

KodePull positions itself as enterprise-quality engineering for businesses that can't afford enterprise contracts. Six service areas: web development, custom software, infrastructure and cloud, mobile, AI and automation, and consulting and advisory. Our client builds so far span several industries. The work with the most pull right now is cloud, custom software, and senior consulting, where the "enterprise-quality but not enterprise-contract" pitch lands hardest.

Our shape is small by design. Fernando and I lead, and we bring in specialist engineers, designers, and other partners based on what each project needs. We don't try to be a sixty-person agency.

## My role

As CTO I own the technical side: architecture, infrastructure design, the hands-on engineering on projects that need it, platform and vendor decisions, and the quality bar we ship under. Fernando owns client relationships, product strategy, and the work of making sure what we ship maps to real business outcomes. We're built to overlap on most decisions and disagree out loud when we should.

## Where it's at now

The latest deliveries were mobile app work and a run of web design and deployment projects. Under them sits the recurring work: maintenance, domain renewals, and hosting. Small and steady, and the base that lets us be selective about the bigger builds.

The hosting runs on our own Kubernetes clusters. Production lives on clusters in the cloud and on bare metal in datacenters. The [on-prem cluster](/engineering/lab/overview) that grew out of my homelab runs the workloads of clients who want their data kept on local hardware, and is the relay and backup site for the production clusters.
