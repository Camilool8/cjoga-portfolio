# Surface brief: portfolio homepage (cjoga.cloud `/` and `/es`)

Mode: Experience with a Persuade close. Audience: hiring managers for Senior/Lead Platform/DevOps roles moving toward AI automation. Primary action: download the CV.

## Approach: refine the incumbent (decided 2026-10-28)
The owner keeps the current look: the Chiang-lineage layout, Syne/Outfit/JetBrains Mono, the mint #64ffda accent, the animations, both themes, and the space/cinematic feel. The earlier "Launch webcast" replacement was rejected; it is stashed and must not return.

The owner ranked these references: Linear, Turion, Rocket Lab, Vast, SpaceX. The influences adopted:
- **Turion orbital scene:** the hero's generic orbs and grid become an orbital backdrop: a planet limb with banded rings, dotted orbits whose dashes drift, satellites at different periods, and probes on transfer arcs. It is pure graphics and motion, with no labels or data (owner, 2026-10-28). Kubestronaut is not the identity thread.
- **Linear restraint:** two-tone headings (a primary sentence that continues in muted text), hairline column grids, FIG-style captions, real terminal/cluster shots as visuals, softer glows.
- **Rocket Lab counters:** boxed odometer counters for certifications, services and apps; thin accent hairlines between sections.
- **Vast/SpaceX chapters:** a ghost wordmark behind a section (the word to be decided with the owner; not Kubestronaut by default), and one full-bleed chapter for the homelab and live terminal.
- Accent stays mint.

Sequence: prototype the hero first and get the owner's reaction, then roll out to the other sections, including the earlier critique fixes (contrast, sub-12px text, a11y defects, the Contact ending, Experience density).
